Find your medicine at a pharmacy near you.

BeysFahi shows which pharmacies in the Maldives have the medicine you need, using each pharmacy's live stock, so you know where to go before you leave home.

Search by name

See which nearby pharmacies have it, whether they're open, and call or get directions.

Scan a prescription

Take a photo and BeysFahi finds every medicine on it. BeysFahi doesn't keep the image.

Live stock

Pharmacies connect their own inventory systems, so stock stays up to date.
